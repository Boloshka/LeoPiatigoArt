About

Leo Piatigo is a contemporary painter working between landscape and figurative traditions. His practice centers on slow looking, tonal restraint, and the emotional resonance of dusk light.

Working primarily in oil, with charcoal as a parallel studio language, he develops bodies of work that sit between observation and memory.

Exhibitions include independent project spaces and private collections across Europe and North America.
